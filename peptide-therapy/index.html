Common questions

Do I need labs before I start?

Yes. Peptide therapy is not prescribed here without an examination and bloodwork. That is the difference between this and an online order form.

Which peptides do you use?

That is a clinical decision made at your visit, based on your exam and your labs, and it is not something to shop for in advance. We will talk through the specifics with you in person.

Can this be done by telehealth?

The initial workup involves an examination and labs, so that happens in the office. Some follow-up visits can be done by video for patients in North Carolina and South Carolina.

Is peptide therapy covered by insurance?

No. Call the office for current pricing.

Can I combine this with weight loss or hormone therapy?

Often, yes — and because all of it happens in one clinic with one chart, nothing gets prescribed in isolation. It is discussed at your visit.
