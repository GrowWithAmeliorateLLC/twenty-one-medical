The equipment matters, but so does who is standing next to it. Laser treatments here are delivered inside a medical clinic by staff who work with a provider on site — which is not the case everywhere offering the same services at a similar price.

Laser hair removal

Permanent hair reduction for face, body, legs, back, chest and underarms, for men and women. Treatment runs in a series of sessions spaced several weeks apart, because hair grows in cycles and a laser only affects the follicles that are actively growing at the time.

  • Consultation first, so you get a realistic session count for your hair and skin — not a number off an ad
  • Men's treatments are a substantial part of what we do, including back and chest
  • Sessions are scheduled in a series; how many depends on the area and on you

Skin rejuvenation

Laser skin treatments for tone, texture and general sun damage. What is appropriate depends on your skin type and what is actually bothering you, which is decided at the consultation rather than picked off a list.

Common questions

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the area, your hair, and your skin. Anyone who gives you a number before looking at you is guessing. You will get a realistic range at the consultation.

Does it hurt?

Most people describe it as a snap rather than a burn, and it is brief. Sensitivity varies by area.

Can men be treated?

Yes, and a good share of our laser hair removal patients are men — back, chest and shoulders in particular.

What do I need to do before a session?

You will get specific instructions at the consultation. In general: avoid sun exposure on the area, and do not wax or pluck beforehand — the laser needs the follicle intact.
