Meet your provider

Twenty years in orthopedic surgery. Then this practice.

Shawn O'Keefe spent two decades in orthopedic surgery before opening Twenty-One Medical. That background is why the wellness side of this clinic runs on labs, examination and follow-up rather than a questionnaire and a prescription.

It is also why one clinic can cover a child's sports physical, a DOT exam, a torn meniscus and a hormone program without sending you anywhere else.
