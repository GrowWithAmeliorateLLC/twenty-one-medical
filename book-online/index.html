Request your appointment

Pick a visit type, choose a time that works, and send the request. The office confirms by email — usually quickly, but this is a request rather than an instant booking, so please wait for the confirmation before making the trip.

Scheduling is handled by DrChrono, our electronic health record. Your details go straight into the clinic's system. If the scheduler does not load for you, call 704-227-5060 and we will book you over the phone.

What you can book yourself

Most visits can be requested online, including new-patient visits, established-patient follow-ups, telehealth, annual wellness visits, physicals, pre-op appointments, DOT physicals, weight-loss follow-ups and aesthetic consultations.

Two things are scheduled by the office instead: hormone procedures and IV procedures. Both need equipment and a block of time set aside, and there is one provider in the building — so those are arranged after your initial consultation rather than dropped into an open slot.

Friday lab draws run from a separate schedule. Select the lab office in the widget if that is what you need.

If you need to be seen today

Walk in. Urgent care visits do not require an appointment during office hours. For anything you think may be an emergency — chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, head injury or stroke symptoms — go to an emergency room or call 911.

Rather just call?

Call 704-227-5060 during office hours and speak to someone. Some things are faster that way.

Before you request

  • Have your date of birth and a working email to hand
  • Choose the visit type that fits — if you are unsure, pick New Patient
  • Watch for the confirmation email before you travel
