Before you call

The quick answers.

Do I need an appointment for urgent care?

No. Walk in during office hours.

How do I get a prescription refilled?

Call the office. For weight-loss medication, request a Weight Loss Follow Up / Medication Pick Up visit online.

How do I get my lab results?

Results are reviewed at a follow-up visit so someone can actually explain them. Call if you have not heard back when you expected to.

Is this an emergency room?

No. For chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, head injury or stroke symptoms, go to an emergency room or call 911.
