Most people end up with a primary care office, an urgent care they use when that office cannot see them, an orthopedist across town, and somewhere else entirely for wellness. Four sets of records, four waiting rooms, four people who do not talk to each other.

Twenty-One Medical covers all of it in one place, with one provider and one chart.

DOT physicals

Certified Department of Transportation exams for commercial drivers, usually available the same week. DOT physicals are not billed to insurance — call for the current cash price.

Telehealth

Video visits are available for patients in North Carolina and South Carolina, for the visit types that do not need hands or equipment. Request a telehealth follow-up online and select the video visit option.

Not sure which visit you need? Call 704-227-5060 and describe what is going on. Someone will tell you honestly whether it is a walk-in, an appointment, or an emergency room.

Common questions

Do you take insurance?

Insurance is accepted for many clinical visits. Some services — DOT physicals, IV therapy and most aesthetic treatments — are not covered by insurance. Call the office and we will check your specific plan.

Can I use you as my primary care provider?

Yes. Request a “New Patient — Establishing Care” visit online to start.

What ages do you see?

From about age five upward.

When should I go to an emergency room instead?

Chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, head injury, stroke symptoms, or anything you think might be life-threatening — go to an ER or call 911. Do not wait for us.
